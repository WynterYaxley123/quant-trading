## What a later preregistration must define

1. Economic mechanism and signed expected relation by horizon, acknowledging that V1/V2 inspired it.
2. Source provenance/licensing/PIT and an admission-time universe; no retrospective completeness selection using future observations.
3. Exact target/scaling/intercept/factor order, mature exchange-session cutoff and deterministic ties.
4. A finite model/selection budget, fixed calendar blocks, drop/null rules and multiple-choice accounting.
5. The full union of already seen training/outcome information, earliest admissible future observations and inaccessible outcome access controls.
6. A genuinely independent Validation budget with success/failure and stability requirements; Final OOS separated and untouched until Validation passes.
7. Public merge and immutable bytes before outcome access; consumed claims cannot be reset after a crash/failure.
8. No forward/live/ETF activity until separately authorized and scientifically eligible.

These requirements describe design review only; no protocol document is generated.
