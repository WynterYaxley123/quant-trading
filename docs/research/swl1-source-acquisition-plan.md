# SWL1 source acquisition plan

Readiness is C / DATA_SOURCE_NOT_READY. Selection is performance-blind: rights,
provenance, historical availability, factual completeness and operability decide
priority. No RankIC, strategy return, model tuning or sealed outcomes are inputs.

| Priority | Exact missing proof | Lawful route | Gate unlocked |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Owner-specific SWS storage/research/automation/derivation/retention rights | [SWS request](source-qualification/vendor-evidence-requests/sws.md), official data-product sales | Rights only; not automatic admission |
| 1 | Original full-universe constituent and hierarchy snapshots, publication/receipt times and revisions | Same SWS archive request, with exact historical scope | PIT for a new generation; no old tier rewrite |
| 1 | Approved TDX endpoints and owner-specific data grant | [TDX request](source-qualification/vendor-evidence-requests/tdx.md) | Price/event rights |
| 1 | Sina hfq endpoint scope and permitted derived use | [Sina request](source-qualification/vendor-evidence-requests/sina.md) | Adjustment-source rights |
| 2 | BaoStock sublicensing, versions, complete lifecycle and factor conventions | [BaoStock request](source-qualification/vendor-evidence-requests/baostock.md) | Conditional documentation becomes reviewable dataset evidence |
| 2 | Independent authorized fact reconciliation and blind numerical quality receipts | After two source grants; trusted authority only | Quality, identity and revision gates |
| 1 | Approved production approver, signing trust and external durable receipts | Owner governance decision | Production identity |

First minimal owner action: approve sending the SWS packet to its official sales
route. No email has been sent. Ask for a written scope/availability response first;
any purchase, contract acceptance, account creation or identity submission requires
separate owner authorization. All prices are QUOTE_REQUIRED; no cost is invented.

Historical scope to request is the frozen published audit interval
2021-08-02 through 2026-09-30, plus the provider's original version boundary and
ongoing observations after a separately approved future protocol. Dates define
the enquiry only; they do not authorize any new strategy or numeric read.

Provider replies belong in an explicitly owner-authorized private evidence store,
outside Git. Public exports contain reviewed metadata, hashes and concise claims
only. Do not mount private grants/keys to workers or post full protected documents.
Grant authority must independently bind actual source generation and contract;
an email or public page cannot become a self-asserted verified=true receipt.

Once rights exist, run blind QA under the authority: full session/identifier/member
coverage, effective intervals, duplicate/conflicting facts, adjusted-price mechanics,
listing/delisting/suspension events and truly independent upstream origin. Expose
nonperformance verdicts and retain immutable access receipts. QA never restores
unseen status for data already accessed, nor creates automatic research activation.

Use Linux Docker for the established synthetic isolation boundary. Windows native
worker containment is optional separately scoped engineering; it is not a task
the owner must complete merely to use the Linux route.
